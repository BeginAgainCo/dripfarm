**Settled**{.badge .settled} · Not a generative avatar or a fantasy version of you.

The base is the real person under showroom conditions, not a synthesized model wearing your face. The trust of "that's actually me, in that actual jacket" is the product. A prettier stranger is a different product.

**Settled**{.badge .settled} · Not a shopping catalogue or a marketplace.

Dripfarm dresses the clothes you already own from your own photos. Recommendations, resale, and store inventory are someone else's product — possibly a later layer on top of ours, never the foundation.

**Leaning**{.badge .leaning} · Not a physics/cloth simulator — at least not first.

Garments are registered pixel overlays, not draped cloth solved against a body mesh. Simulation is a quality upgrade we can buy per-layer later if and where it earns its cost. The bet is that clean layers beat simulated drape for the *Clueless* moment.

**Leaning**{.badge .leaning} · Not committed to any one image engine.

The base pipeline and the overlay renderer are roles, not products. Which background remover, which relighting model, which compositor fills each role is deliberately undecided — see [Phase 1](#moves).

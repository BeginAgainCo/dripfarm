**Settled**{.badge .settled} · Dripfarm is a virtual closet that dresses *you*, layer by layer.

You give it photos — a base shot of yourself and your OOTD ("outfit of the day") pictures. It separates those into reusable parts: a clean base layer of you, individual garments, hairstyles, and makeup looks. Then it lets you re-stack any combination and see it on your own body.

**Settled**{.badge .settled} · The four part-types are: base, garments, hair, and face.

- **Base** — one clean, showroom-grade image of the person. The canvas.
- **Garments** — separated clothing pieces, each with an array of *wear variants* (tucked / untucked, sleeves up / down, open / closed).
- **Hair** — a switchable style with a **front** layer and a **back** layer that bracket the garment stack.
- **Face** — switchable makeup looks applied to the face region.

**Leaning**{.badge .leaning} · A garment is not one image — it's a small set of ways it can be worn.

The same shirt worn open over a tee and worn buttoned-and-tucked are different overlays of the same garment. We model that as an array of wear variants on the garment, so styling means picking pieces *and* picking how each is worn.

**Settled**{.badge .settled} · The output is the *Clueless* moment: pick parts, see the look, judge it in a glance.

The reference is exact — Cher's closet software that mixes and matches outfits and shows the result on a rotating photo of her. Dripfarm is that, with real separated layers instead of a canned slideshow.

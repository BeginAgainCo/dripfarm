The closet in *Clueless* was the future. **Dripfarm is that closet, made real — with your own clothes and your own face.**

**Settled**{.badge .settled} · A person's wardrobe is worth digitizing as *layers*, not as a flat grid of product shots.

Every wardrobe app since 1995 has chased Cher's rotating outfit-matcher and stopped at a photo gallery. The interesting part was never the gallery — it was seeing *yourself* wearing the combination before you put it on. That only works if the clothes, the hair, and the face are separable layers that re-composite on demand.

**Settled**{.badge .settled} · The unit of value is "a dressable you," built once and re-used forever.

A clean base image of the person plus a library of separated garments, hairstyles, and makeup looks. From those parts the app can render any outfit — without a new photoshoot for every combination. The photoshoot happens once; the styling happens infinitely.
